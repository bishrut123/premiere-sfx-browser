SFX Browser — a Premiere Pro panel for your own sound effects library
Works with Premiere Pro 2022 or newer, on macOS and Windows.

MAC
  1. Double-click "install-mac.command".
     If macOS says it can't be opened: open System Settings > Privacy & Security,
     scroll down and click "Open Anyway", then double-click the file again.
  2. Quit Premiere Pro (Cmd+Q) and reopen it.
  3. Window > Extensions > SFX Browser.

WINDOWS
  1. Double-click "install-windows.bat".
     If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC": click "More info" > "Run anyway".
  2. Close Premiere Pro and reopen it.
  3. Window > Extensions > SFX Browser.

FIRST USE
  Click the + button in the panel and choose your sound effects folder.

UPDATING
  Download the new version and run the installer again. Your library folders and
  settings are kept.

UNINSTALL
  Mac:     delete  ~/Library/Application Support/Adobe/CEP/extensions/sfx-browser
  Windows: delete  %APPDATA%\Adobe\CEP\extensions\sfx-browser

More info: https://github.com/bishrut123/premiere-sfx-browser
